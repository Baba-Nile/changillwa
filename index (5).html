Current Reality

Challenges Facing Kabiro Ward

Maxwell Changilwa has walked these streets, felt these problems firsthand — and has a clear plan to fix every one of them.

🛣️

Poor Roads & Infrastructure

Key roads like Salim Road remain unmaintained. Maxwell pledges a modernized Salim Rd and upgraded street infrastructure that serves every resident.

💼

Youth Unemployment

Thousands of Kabiro youth remain jobless. Political brokers have handed out hundreds instead of building real opportunity. That stops in 2027.

🔒

Insecurity

Maxwell himself lost his phone to a motorbike snatcher in Kabiro. Two operational police posts will end theft and make our ward truly safe.

💧

Lack of Clean Water

Free water at Muslim Primary was shut down by the current leadership. Maxwell will restore and expand free water points across Kabiro every Sunday.

🚽

Poor Sanitation

Kabiro's streets are dirty not because residents don't care — but because political brokers have failed to implement proper waste management solutions.

🏗️

Neglected Mjengo Workers

The mjengo community made Maxwell who he is. He will never stop advocating for safe conditions, fair wages, and social protection for all site workers.

🏥

Healthcare Emergencies

Maxwell nearly died from food poisoning on a Sunday when all private hospitals in Kabiro were closed. A dispensary with an emergency ambulance is non-negotiable.

🎓

Unfair Bursary Distribution

Bursaries have not reached the right students. Maxwell pledges equal bursary distribution — your voter's card is your pass. Register as a Kabiro voter today.

⚽

No Sports Facilities

If Kilimani Primary has a modernized football field, Kabiro Primary and Muslim Primary will get one too. Our youth deserve proper grounds to grow their talent.

Manifesto Highlights

A people's plan born from listening — to market traders, mothers, mjengo workers, youth, and elders of Kabiro Ward.

🔒

Security

  • 2 operational police posts in Kabiro
  • End motorbike theft & street crime
  • Youth diversion from crime pathways
  • Community policing mobilization
🏥

Healthcare

  • Fully equipped ward dispensary
  • Emergency response ambulance
  • Subsidized medication access
  • Free quarterly medical camps
💧

Water & Sanitation

  • Free water: Muslim Primary, Kabiro Primary, St Anthony & Raila Estate
  • TakaSafi TakaKazi waste program
