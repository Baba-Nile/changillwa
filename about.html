Biography

Kabiro Shaped Who I Am

Hon. Maxwell Changilwa Amugune is a son of Kabiro — shaped by the mjengo community, the mama mbogas, and the everyday struggles of the people he has always lived among. Every rough road he has walked, every site worker he has spoken to, every mother he has listened to is his reason for running for MCA, Kabiro Ward, Dagoretti North, in the 2027 elections.

Maxwell is the founding voice of the Official Maxwell Changilwa Amugune Supporters Community and advocates for a safe Kabiro — Bila Political Brokers. He believes an MCA must be close to the people, like a mzee wa kijiji, not hidden behind gatekeepers and middlemen. For too long, vijana have struggled to get two hundred shillings from their MCA, believing it is an entitlement, while elders cannot focus on real development. That culture must change.

He has personally lived the healthcare crisis in Kabiro — nearly dying from food poisoning on a Sunday when every private hospital in the ward was closed and no emergency response was available. He has lost his phone to a motorbike snatcher right here in Kabiro. These are not statistics to Maxwell — they are the fire behind every pledge in his manifesto.

Core Values

What Maxwell Stands For

🔒
Safety for All

Kabiro needs more than streetlights — it needs operational police posts and security personnel on the ground every day, in every corner of the ward.

🚫
No Political Brokers

Resources, bursaries, and development belong to the residents of Kabiro — not to brokers and middlemen. Maxwell pledges direct, transparent leadership for all.

🤝
Servant Leadership

An MCA should be close to the people — like a mzee wa kijiji. Always accessible, always present, always accountable to every Kabiro resident.

👷
Worker Dignity

The mjengo community is the family that made Maxwell who he is. He will never stop fighting for fair wages, site safety, and social protection for every worker.

⚖️
Equal Distribution

Bursaries, free water, healthcare, and development must reach every Kabiro resident equally — your voter's card is your right, not a favour from a broker.

🙏
Faith & Integrity

Prayers have lifted Maxwell above every challenge. He leads with honesty, humility, and a deep commitment to serve — not to be served.

Leadership Journey

From Streets to Service

Maxwell's path to leadership was built through years of grassroots organizing, personal sacrifice, and an unshakeable belief that Kabiro can — and will — be better.

Roots
Born and Raised in Kabiro

Grew up in Kabiro Ward, walking the same unpaved roads, witnessing the same neglect, and feeling the same daily struggles as every resident. Kabiro did not just shape him — it became his mandate.

Community
Mjengo Community Member & Advocate

Connected deeply with Kabiro's construction worker community — the family that made him who he is. Began speaking out against exploitation, unsafe working conditions, and the absence of social protection for site workers across Nairobi.

Organizing
Grassroots Mobilizer

Organized Kabiro residents around shared challenges — insecurity, poor roads, lack of clean water, unequal bursary distribution, and absent healthcare. Championed free water initiatives that the current leadership later shut down.

Conviction
Lived Every Crisis He Fights Against

Nearly died from food poisoning on a Sunday when no local emergency response was available in Kabiro. Lost his phone to a motorbike snatcher in his own ward. These personal moments became the strongest arguments for his candidacy.

2025 — Present
Official MCA Campaign — Kabiro Ward 2027

Launched the official campaign for MCA, Kabiro Ward, Dagoretti North, in the 2027 general elections — with a people's manifesto rooted in security, healthcare, free water, equal bursaries, mjengo worker rights, and a Kabiro Bila Political Brokers.

Pledges to the People

What Maxwell Will Deliver

🔒 2 fully operational police posts in Kabiro to end theft, phone snatching, and daily insecurity in the ward
🏥 A fully equipped ward dispensary with a dedicated emergency response ambulance available 24/7 — no more Sunday crises
💧 Restore and expand free water every Sunday at Muslim Primary, Kabiro Primary, St Anthony, and Raila Estate
🎓 Equal bursary distribution to all Kabiro voters and residents — your voter's card is your bursary pass, usichelewe
👷 Relentless advocacy for every mjengo worker's rights — fair wages, site safety, NHIF, NSSF, and a Mjengo SACCO
🏗️ TakaSafi TakaKazi — hire Kabiro youth to manage waste, keep our mtaa clean, and protect our roads
🛣️ Modernized Salim Road, full-night solar street lights, and modern football fields at Kabiro Primary and Muslim Primary

Personal Message

Every rough road I've walked, every mjengo worker I've spoken to, every mama mboga I've listened to — they are my reason for running. My leadership will be measured not by speeches, but by the lives we change together."

— Hon. Maxwell Changilwa Amugune, MCA Aspirant, Kabiro Ward
