The Biggest Failure

What Kabiro Was Promised & Never Got

🚨 Biggest Failure

The Roads That Were Never Fixed

For years, Kabiro residents have been told that road repairs are coming. Money has been allocated through county budgets. Promises were made during every election cycle. Yet the roads — including key routes like Salim Road — remain in the same condition or worse. Funds meant for Kabiro infrastructure have not reached the ground.

📜 Constitution of Kenya, Article 43(1)(d)

"Every person has the right to accessible and adequate housing, and to reasonable standards of sanitation" — this extends to the public infrastructure that connects communities to services, opportunity, and dignity.

❌Salim Road — promised for years, still unmaintained and flooded each rainy season
❌Solar street lights — budgeted for but never installed or installed and left broken
❌Drainage systems — flooding continues in the same spots year after year with no resolution

⚖️ What the Law Says

The Public Finance Management Act requires that county funds be used transparently, for the purposes they were allocated, with measurable outcomes reported to residents. When road funds are allocated but roads remain broken, that is not just a failure of leadership — it may be a violation of law.

Maxwell Changilwa Amugune pledges quarterly public accountability forums where every shilling of ward development fund spending is reported openly to Kabiro residents — no exceptions.

✅ Maxwell's Pledge

A modernized Salim Road. Full-night solar street lights with no interruptions. Proper drainage across every flooding hotspot. These are not promises — they are the first items on the accountability checklist when Maxwell is elected in 2027.

🔒 Security

No Police Posts — Residents Left Unprotected

Kabiro Ward has no operational police post despite persistent insecurity. Phone snatching on motorbikes, theft, and crime continue unchecked. Streetlights were promised as the solution — they were never enough and were never fully delivered.

📜 Article 29 — Constitution of Kenya

"Every person has the right to freedom and security of the person, which includes the right not to be subjected to… violence from either public or private sources."

✅ Maxwell pledges 2 operational police posts
Abandoned
💧 Water

Free Water Initiative Shut Down by Current Leader

A free water programme at Muslim Primary School was serving Kabiro residents — until the current MCA stopped it. Access to clean water is a constitutional right. Withdrawing a free water service from the poorest residents is a betrayal of public office and of the people.

📜 Article 43(1)(d) — Constitution of Kenya

"Every person has the right to clean and safe water in adequate quantities."

✅ Maxwell will restore & expand free water at 4 locations
Failed
🏥 Healthcare

No Dispensary. No Ambulance. People Dying Needlessly.

Kabiro has no ward-level dispensary with emergency capacity. On Sundays, all private clinics close. When Maxwell nearly died from food poisoning and had to travel kilometres for help, he knew this was not just his crisis — it is every Kabiro resident's crisis, every single day.

📜 Article 43(1)(a) — Constitution of Kenya

"Every person has the right to the highest attainable standard of health, which includes the right to health care services, including reproductive health care."

✅ Maxwell pledges a dispensary + emergency ambulance
Failed
🌿 Sanitation

Kabiro's Cleanliness Held Hostage by Political Brokers

Kabiro is not dirty because residents do not care — Kabiro is dirty because the current leadership has failed to implement proper, sustainable waste management. Political brokers control waste contracts. Youth who could be employed cleaning their own mtaa are jobless instead.

📜 Article 42 — Constitution of Kenya

"Every person has the right to a clean and healthy environment, which includes the right to have the environment protected for the benefit of present and future generations."

✅ Maxwell pledges TakaSafi TakaKazi — youth-led waste jobs
Failed
🎓 Education

Bursaries Captured by Political Networks — Not Students

Bursary funds meant for Kabiro's needy students have been distributed unevenly — favour going to political allies rather than the most deserving residents. Students from the right family or the right political camp get support. Others go without. This is a theft of opportunity from Kabiro's children.

📜 Article 53(1)(b) — Constitution of Kenya

"Every child has the right to free and compulsory basic education." County bursaries exist to ensure no child is denied education due to poverty — not to reward political loyalty.

✅ Maxwell pledges equal bursaries — your voter's card is your pass
Failed
💼 Youth Empowerment

Youth Handed Mia Mbili Instead of Opportunity

The current system hands young people two hundred shillings during campaigns and calls it empowerment. This culture — fuelled by brokers — has made youth dependent on handouts rather than connected to real jobs, skills, and development. Vijana deserve more than being bought for a day.

📜 Article 55 — Constitution of Kenya

"The State shall take measures, including affirmative action programmes, to ensure that the youth access relevant education and training; have opportunities to associate, be represented and participate in political, social, economic and other spheres of life."

✅ Maxwell pledges sports fields, SACCOs & real job pathways
Abandoned
⚽ Sports & Talent

No Modern Football Fields While Kilimani Has Everything

Neighbouring wards like Kilimani have modernized football pitches and youth facilities. Kabiro Primary and Muslim Primary have nothing comparable. Talent goes to waste daily because no one invested in the infrastructure our youth need to grow, compete, and earn a living through sport.

📜 Article 11 — Constitution of Kenya

"The State shall promote all forms of national and cultural expression through literature, the arts, traditional celebrations, science, communication, information, mass media, publications, libraries and other cultural heritage." Sport is part of that expression — and Kabiro's youth deserve their share.

✅ Maxwell pledges modern fields at Kabiro & Muslim Primary
Failed
💡 Infrastructure

Street Lights — Promised, Half-Built, Left to Fail

Street lights were promised as a security solution for Kabiro. Some were installed — then left to break, flicker, and go dark with no maintenance plan or accountability. Residents remain unsafe at night. A light that is off is no different from a light that was never there.

📜 Article 10 — National Values & Principles of Governance

"The national values and principles of governance include… good governance, integrity, transparency and accountability." Half-finished public works with no follow-up maintenance violate every one of these values.

✅ Maxwell pledges full-night solar lights — zero interruptions
Abandoned
👷 Workers Rights

Mjengo Workers — Used, Then Forgotten

Construction workers in Kabiro build Nairobi's skyline without contracts, insurance, fair pay, or any MCA speaking for them in the county assembly. No ward-level leader has ever organized a SACCO, a legal clinic, or an NHIF drive specifically for Kabiro's mjengo community. They have been invisible to leadership — but not to Maxwell.

📜 Article 41 — Constitution of Kenya

"Every person has the right to fair labour practices." This includes every mjengo worker in Kabiro — and an MCA who stays silent while workers are exploited is complicit in that exploitation.

✅ Maxwell pledges a Mjengo SACCO, NHIF drives & legal clinics
