The Family That Made Me — I Will Never Stop Fighting For You

The mjengo community is the family that made Maxwell Changilwa Amugune who he is. Construction workers build the skylines of Nairobi — yet they remain among the most exploited, unprotected, and forgotten workers in Kenya. That ends with this campaign.

The Reality

What Mjengo Workers Face Every Day

Kabiro's construction workers power Nairobi's growth — but they go home with broken bodies, stolen wages, and no safety nets. Maxwell has seen this firsthand, and he refuses to look away.

💰
Wage Theft & Exploitation

Contractors routinely withhold or slash agreed pay. Workers have no written contracts, making legal recourse nearly impossible. This is not just unfair — it is criminal.

⛑️
No Safety Equipment

Most mjengo sites lack helmets, harnesses, and first aid kits. Falls and injuries are common — and workers are left to foot their own medical bills with no support from contractors.

🏥
No Healthcare or Insurance

The majority of construction workers are unregistered — no NHIF, no NSSF. A single accident can push an entire family into poverty with zero protection from the state or employer.

📄
No Formal Contracts

Workers are hired verbally, worked casually, and dismissed without notice or compensation. Without contracts, they have no legal standing when things go wrong on site.

🕐
Excessive Working Hours

Long shifts with no overtime pay are standard. Workers endure this in silence, fearing they will be replaced if they speak up. Fear is the contractor's most powerful tool.

🏚️
No Voice, No Representation

For too long, no leader in Kabiro has sat down with mjengo workers, listened to them, and brought their voices into the county assembly. Maxwell is changing that — starting now.

Maxwell's Pledge

Never Stopping Advocacy for Workers

Maxwell Changilwa Amugune has always stood with the mjengo community — this is the family that made him. As MCA, he will use every available legal and civic channel to protect, represent, and uplift every construction worker in Kabiro Ward.

✅Fair Wage Advocacy: Push for county-level minimum wage enforcement for all construction workers in Nairobi including Kabiro
✅Contract Enforcement: Lobby for mandatory written contracts for all casual and permanent mjengo workers on every site
✅Safety Standards: Advocate for building inspection requirements that include full worker safety compliance as a condition of site approval
✅NHIF & NSSF Enrollment: Organize mass registration drives to enroll every Kabiro mjengo worker into social protection systems
✅Mjengo Workers SACCO: Facilitate a dedicated Kabiro Mjengo SACCO for savings, emergency loans, and collective bargaining power
✅Legal Clinics: Organize free legal aid sessions for workers to understand and confidently assert their rights against exploitative contractors
✅County Assembly Voice: Bring mjengo worker issues directly to the floor of the Nairobi County Assembly — their problems will no longer be invisible

🔨 Mjengo SACCO Vision

A dedicated Kabiro Mjengo Workers SACCO will give construction workers the financial tools to save, access emergency funds, and build collective wealth — without falling prey to exploitative lenders or contractors.
