The Kabiro Manifesto 2027

Home/Manifesto
Hon. Maxwell Changilwa Amugune — 2027

A People's Plan — Kabiro Bila Political Brokers

This manifesto was born from listening — to mjengo workers, mama mbogas, mothers, youth, and elders. It is a contract with Kabiro residents to Reset what brokers took, Restore your rights, and Rebuild systems that work for everyone.

01
First Priority

Security for Every Resident

Kabiro does not need street lights alone — it needs boots on the ground. Every resident deserves to walk safely in their own ward, day and night.

✅Establish 2 fully operational police posts within Kabiro Ward to deter and respond to crime
✅End motorbike phone snatching and street muggings through increased security personnel presence
✅Mobilize community policing units across all sub-locations
✅Install energy-saving solar street lights along all major roads and alleys — full-night operation, no interruptions
✅Engage the NYS for additional ward security patrols alongside community policing efforts
✅Launch youth diversion programs to address the root causes of crime in the ward
"I lost my phone recently to a phone snatcher on a motorbike right here in Kabiro. If it happened to me, it is happening to our people every day. I pledge to make Kabiro safer — for everyone." — Maxwell Changilwa Amugune
02
Wellbeing

Healthcare Access & Emergency Response

How many Kabiro residents die daily because no emergency healthcare is available? This must stop. A dispensary with a fully equipped emergency ambulance is not a luxury — it is a right.

✅Build a fully equipped dispensary within Kabiro Ward for 24/7 resident access
✅Deploy a dedicated emergency response ambulance to handle crises within Kabiro
✅Ensure subsidized and affordable medication is available to all residents
✅Organize quarterly free medical camps across all sub-locations
✅Facilitate NHIF enrollment drives for uninsured workers and families
✅Support mother and child wellness clinics with essential supplies and trained staff
✅Launch mental health awareness campaigns for youth and families across the ward
"In one of my meetings I nearly died due to food poisoning. It was a Sunday — all private hospitals in Kabiro were closed and no emergency response was available. I had to rush to a hospital kilometers away. If it affected me, it must not affect others. We MUST save our people from such a healthcare crisis." — Maxwell Changilwa Amugune
03
Basic Rights

Free Water for Kabiro Residents

Clean, affordable water is a basic human right. The current leadership shut down free water at Muslim Primary — a service that helped our people. Maxwell will restore it and expand it across the ward.

✅Restore and expand government-subsidized or free water points across all Kabiro sub-locations
✅Weekly free water access every Sunday at Muslim Primary School
✅Weekly free water access every Sunday at Kabiro Primary School
✅Weekly free water access every Sunday at St Anthony
✅Weekly free water access every Sunday at Raila Estate for all residents
✅Upgrade and maintain public sanitation blocks to restore dignity across the ward
"Pale Muslim Primary, we had free water for residents — but the current leader stopped the initiative. He doesn't care. We MUST restore what was taken from our people." — Maxwell Changilwa Amugune
04
TakaSafi TakaKazi

Sanitation & Clean Environment

Kabiro is not dirty because residents don't care — Kabiro is dirty because political brokers have failed to implement real solutions. Vijana wapewe kazi, mtaa ingare.

✅Launch the TakaSafi TakaKazi youth employment program — hire local youth to keep Kabiro clean
✅Implement proper solid waste management with regular collection schedules that do not damage our roads
✅Place strategically located skip bins across all Kabiro sub-locations
✅Create green parks and recreational spaces for community health and wellbeing
✅Upgrade public toilets and sanitation infrastructure to restore dignity in daily life
05
The Family That Made Me

Mjengo Workers Rights

The mjengo community is the family that made Maxwell Changilwa who he is. He will never stop advocating for the rights, safety, and dignity of every site worker in Kabiro and beyond.

✅Champion fair wage ordinances and advocate loudly for every Kabiro construction worker
✅Support the formation of a Mjengo Workers SACCO for savings, loans and collective empowerment
✅Enforce worksite safety standards to eliminate preventable injuries and deaths on site
✅Drive NHIF and NSSF enrollment for all unregistered construction workers and their families
✅Ensure mjengo workers are never exploited — advocate through county assembly for binding protections
